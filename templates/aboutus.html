About Our Platform

Empowering the workforce of Nepal through digital connectivity.

Our Mission

JobMandu was founded with a simple goal: to make job hunting in Nepal transparent and efficient. We connect talented individuals with top-tier companies across the country.

We focus on local opportunities, ensuring that whether you are in Kathmandu, Pokhara, or Biratnagar, your next career move is just a click away.

Our dedicated team works tirelessly to vet employers and job postings, providing a safe and reliable platform for all users.

Our Philosophy

WHAT WE DO

We value long-term relationships; our professionals aim to be upfront with diverse solutions that can generate an atmosphere of trust and confidence for us in the hearts of our clients.

We offer a range of services for our clients which ranges from Job Posting, Permanent placement, Headhunt/executive search, and Management consultation services.

We believe in continuous improvement and innovation, constantly updating our platform to meet the evolving needs of job seekers and employers alike.

At JobMandu, we are more than just a job portal; we are a community dedicated to fostering career growth and development in Nepal.
